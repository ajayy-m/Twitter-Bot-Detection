System Architecture
Classification Pipeline
1
Raw Feature Ingestion
30+ profile & behaviour signals loaded: follower counts, tweet metrics, engagement ratios, profile completeness flags.
2
Feature Engineering
Derived signals: tweets/day, spike scores, log-transforms, young account & popularity flags.
3
Scaling & Normalisation
RobustScaler on tabular features; dedicated scaler on account age. Handles outliers gracefully.
4
Graph Embedding Lookup
k-NN (k=10, cosine) finds similar training accounts; their 64-d embeddings are averaged and L2-normalised.
5
Random Forest Classifier
Full feature vector passed to trained Random Forest. P(Human) score derived from ensemble vote.
6
LIME Explainability
Local surrogate model (300 perturbation samples) identifies the 15 features most responsible for this prediction.
7
LLM Narrative
Top LIME evidence translated into plain-English analyst summary by a local Ollama language model.

Common Bot vs Human Signals
⚡
Follower Spikes
A sudden large follower count on a new account is a strong bot indicator — real accounts grow gradually.
💬
Organic Engagement
Genuine reply and retweet interactions over time suggest authentic human behaviour and community participation.
#️⃣
Hashtag Overuse
Bots often spam hashtags far above the human average in order to artificially amplify trending topics.
✍️
Rich Vocabulary
A diverse range of unique words across tweets is a strong indicator of genuine human-authored content.
🤖
Incomplete Profile
Missing bio, no profile photo, no location, and no website link — common traits of automated accounts.
📅
Established Account Age
Older accounts with consistent activity over years are far less likely to be recently created bots.
